# Blog Editorial Calendar — The Big 5 (They Ask, You Answer)

Marcus Sheridan's premise: rank and earn trust by answering the questions buyers
actually ask — especially the ones competitors avoid. The **Big 5** buckets:
**Cost, Problems, Comparisons, Best-of/Reviews, "Is it worth it".** Every post
targets a real search, cross-links a service + a city page, and ends with a CTA.

Each post's `category` frontmatter must be one of:
`Cost | Comparisons | Problems | Best-of | How-to`.

## Cadence
Aim for **2–4 posts/month.** Front-load Cost and Comparison posts — they convert
and rank fastest. Refresh the "(2026)" cost posts annually (update the year and
numbers; bump `updatedDate`). Add a hero image to each post (`public/images/blog/`).

## Post template checklist
- Primary keyword in title, H1 (title), first paragraph, and slug
- `description` ≤ 155 chars, compelling (it's the SERP snippet)
- `related: [...]` service paths for internal links
- At least one link to a service page + one to a city page in the body
- A clear CTA (quote + phone) at the end
- Honest, specific, local — real numbers, real East-TN conditions
