# Backlink & Local Citation Playbook

Two layers: **on-site internal linking** (already built into the site) and
**off-site link/citation acquisition** (the work below). Do these in order —
citations and Google Business Profile move the local rankings fastest.

## 0. On-site internal linking (done — maintain it)
The site already cross-links hub↔spoke services, service↔city pages, and
blog↔service. When you add a page, wire it into the relevant `related` arrays in
`src/data/services.ts` and link it from any relevant blog post. Every new blog
post should link to at least one service page and one city page.

## 1. Google Business Profile (highest priority)
- Claim/verify GBP for **1201 Bob Kirby Rd, Knoxville** with the exact NAP in `company.ts`.
- Primary category: **Concrete Contractor**. Secondary: Paving Contractor, Masonry Contractor, Deck Builder, Landscaper.
- Fill every field: services (mirror the site's service list), service areas (all city pages), hours, description.
- Add 20+ real project photos; post weekly (new projects, tips) — GBP posts are a ranking + conversion lever.
- Launch a **review generation** habit: text every happy customer the GBP review link. Target steady 5-star flow (competitors like Earthadelic win on this).

## 2. Core citations / NAP consistency (identical NAP everywhere)
Priority directories — claim and keep NAP identical:
- Bing Places, Apple Business Connect
- Yelp, Angi, Houzz, Thumbtack, Porch, HomeAdvisor
- BBB (Better Business Bureau), Nextdoor (page already exists — verify NAP)
- Yellowpages, Superpages, Manta, Foursquare
- Facebook (page live — confirm NAP + services match)

**NAP consistency is a ranking factor.** Fix the "7+ vs 20+ years" discrepancy first so citations don't conflict.

## 3. Niche / industry backlinks (high value)
- **Paver manufacturer contractor locators** — Belgard, Techo-Bloc, Unilock "find a contractor" (become a listed installer; strong topical links competitors use).
- **ICPI / NCMA** (Interlocking Concrete Pavement Institute / concrete masonry) certifications + directory listings.
- Concrete/hardscape supplier partner pages (your local aggregate/paver supplier).

## 4. Local / community links
- Knoxville, Farragut, Maryville, Oak Ridge **Chambers of Commerce** (membership = directory link).
- **Home Builders Association of Greater Knoxville** membership + directory.
- Local sponsorships (youth sports, community events) → sponsor pages link back.
- Local news / lifestyle (Cityview, Knox News) — pitch project stories, "best of" nominations.

## 5. Link-earning content (already seeded, keep building)
The blog's cost guides, comparisons and "problem" posts are the link magnets —
other local sites and forums cite honest cost data. Build out the editorial
calendar, add original project photography, and publish local case studies
(before/after with the problem solved). These earn links passively.

## 6. Review + reputation platforms
Beyond GBP: Facebook reviews, Houzz reviews, Angi. Feature them on `/reviews/`
(already schema-marked) — this compounds trust and CTR.

## Tracking
Keep a simple sheet: Directory | URL | NAP submitted | Status | Login. Audit
NAP consistency quarterly with a tool (BrightLocal, Moz Local, or manual).
