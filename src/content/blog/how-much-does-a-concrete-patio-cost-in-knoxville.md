A concrete patio is the most affordable way to add real outdoor living space to your Knoxville home.
Here's what one costs in **2026**, and what moves the number.

## The short answer

- **Broom-finish concrete patio:** ~$8–$15 per square foot installed
- **Stamped / decorative concrete patio:** ~$12–$20+ per square foot installed

A common 300 sq ft patio runs roughly **$2,400–$4,500 broom** or **$3,600–$6,000+ stamped.**

## What drives the price

- **Finish.** Plain broom is cheapest; stamped patterns, integral color and borders cost more (and look like stone at a fraction of paver prices).
- **Grading & drainage.** The patio has to shed water away from your foundation. Tricky lots that need regrading or drainage add cost — but skipping it causes pooling and cracking.
- **Base prep & thickness.** Proper compacted base and reinforcement are what keep a patio from cracking across the middle.
- **Site access & tear-out.** Removing an old patio or hauling material through tight access adds labor.
- **Features.** Steps, seat walls and fire-feature pads add to the total.

## Concrete vs. pavers for a patio

Concrete is the value choice; a [paver patio](/blog/how-much-does-a-paver-patio-cost-in-knoxville/) costs more (~$18–$35/sq ft) but flexes with the ground and is repairable. If you want the stone look without paver pricing, [stamped concrete](/concrete/decorative-and-stamped/) splits the difference.
