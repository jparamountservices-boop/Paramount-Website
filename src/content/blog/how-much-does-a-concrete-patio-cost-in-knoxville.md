A concrete patio is the most affordable way to add real outdoor living space to your Knoxville home.
Here's what one costs in **2026**, and what moves the number.

## The short answer

- **Broom-finish or troweled concrete patio:** ~$10–$12 per square foot installed
- **Stamped / stained decorative concrete patio:** upwards of ~$18–$20 per square foot installed

A common 300 sq ft patio runs roughly **$3,000–$3,600 broom** or **$5,400–$6,000+ stamped.**

## What drives the price

- **Finish.** Plain broom is cheapest; stamped patterns, integral color and borders cost more (and look like stone at a fraction of paver prices).
- **Grading & drainage.** The patio has to shed water away from your foundation. Tricky lots that need regrading or drainage add cost — but skipping it causes pooling and cracking.
- **Base prep, thickness & mix.** Proper compacted base and reinforcement — fiber, wire mesh and a higher-PSI mix — are what keep a patio from cracking across the middle.
- **Site access & tear-out.** Removing an old patio, hauling material through tight access, or having to pump the concrete to a hard-to-reach spot all add labor.
- **Features.** Steps, seat walls and fire-feature pads add to the total.

## Concrete vs. pavers for a patio

Concrete is the value choice; a [paver patio](/blog/how-much-does-a-paver-patio-cost-in-knoxville/) costs more (~$25–$35/sq ft) but flexes with the ground and is repairable. If you want the stone look without paver pricing, [stamped concrete](/concrete/decorative-and-stamped/) splits the difference.
