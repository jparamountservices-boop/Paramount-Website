A paver patio is one of the best returns you can get on your backyard — but the price range online is
all over the map. Here's what one actually costs in **Knoxville in 2026**, and what drives the
number.

## The short answer

Most paver patios in the Knoxville area run about **$25 to $35 per square foot installed.** A common
300–400 sq ft patio typically lands between **$7,500 and $14,000**, depending on the paver you
choose, the pattern, and any walls, steps or fire features.

By size (field patio, mid-range paver):

- **200 sq ft:** ~$5,000–$7,000
- **400 sq ft:** ~$10,000–$14,000
- **600+ sq ft with features:** $18,000 and up

## Where the money goes

The pavers themselves are only part of the cost. Here's the real breakdown:

- **Base work (the big one).** A paver patio is only as good as its base — excavation, a deep
  compacted crushed-stone base, and a screeded bedding layer. This is the labor that makes it last 50
  years, and it's the first thing cheap installs skimp on.
- **Paver choice.** Standard concrete pavers cost less; premium Belgard, Techo-Bloc and Regal lines,
  or natural stone, cost more.
- **Pattern & cuts.** Herringbone, circles, borders and lots of curved cuts add labor.
- **Edge restraint & polymeric sand.** Non-negotiable for a patio that stays tight and weed-free.
- **Features.** Seat walls, fire pits, steps and lighting each add to the total —
  but they’re also what turns a patio into an outdoor room.

## Pavers vs. concrete on cost

Yes, pavers cost more up front than a poured concrete patio (~$10–$12/sq ft broom-finished). But they flex with our
clay soil instead of cracking, last longer, and are repairable one unit at a time. If you're weighing
