Replacing a driveway is a big decision, and the first fork in the road is **concrete or pavers.**
Since we install both, here's the straight comparison for East Tennessee.

## Cost

- **Concrete driveway:** ~$6–$12 per square foot
- **Paver driveway:** ~$45–$55 per square foot

Concrete is clearly the lower up-front cost. Pavers cost more because each unit is set by hand on a deep, engineered base.

## Lifespan

- **Concrete:** 30+ years when built on a proper base with drainage.
- **Pavers:** 50+ years — they flex with our clay soil instead of cracking on it.

## Cracking & repairs

This is the big one in East Tennessee, where clay soil moves with freeze-thaw cycles.

- **Concrete** is a rigid slab. Built right it lasts, but ground movement can eventually crack it, and a crack or stain is hard to fix invisibly.
- **Pavers** flex with the ground and are repairable one unit at a time — lift the damaged paver, reset it, done. No patchwork.

## Curb appeal

Concrete offers broom, stamped and decorative finishes. Pavers offer a much wider range of colors, patterns and borders, and many buyers see them as the premium, higher-resale option.

## Maintenance

- **Concrete:** occasional cleaning; reseal decorative finishes every couple of years.
- **Pavers:** occasional joint re-sanding; the trade-off for their flexibility and repairability.

## Bottom line

- Choose **concrete** if up-front budget is the priority and you want a proven, lower-cost driveway.
- Choose **pavers** if you want maximum lifespan, easy repairs and the highest curb appeal — and you plan to stay a while.
