If you're building a paver patio or driveway, you'll run into three names again and again: **Belgard,
Techo-Bloc and Unilock.** All three are premium, warrantied manufacturers we install — so this isn't
about a "bad" brand. It's about matching the right line to your project and budget.

## What they have in common

- **Color-fast, high-strength concrete pavers** engineered to hold up to freeze-thaw — important in East Tennessee.
- **Manufacturer warranties** on the pavers themselves.
- Full systems — matching **pavers, wall block, coping and steps** so a whole project ties together.

## Belgard

The most widely available of the three, with a broad range from budget-friendly to high-end designer
lines. Strong for classic, natural looks and a safe choice when you want proven products and lots of
style options. Often the best **value-to-quality** balance.

## Techo-Bloc

Known for bold, modern designs, striking color blends and a premium finish. If you want a
contemporary, high-design look that stands out, Techo-Bloc's lineup is hard to beat. Typically a
**premium** price point.

## Unilock

A pioneer in the paver industry with proprietary surface technologies (like their color/finish
processes) that deliver excellent durability and a refined look. Great for homeowners who want
**engineered performance** and a distinctive finish.

## How to actually choose

Honestly, the **brand matters less than three other things:**

1. **The specific line** within a brand — each maker has budget and premium tiers.
2. **The look** — pick the color and texture that fits your home; all three deliver on quality.
3. **The installation** — a premium paver on a bad base fails faster than a mid-range paver installed right. Base prep, edge restraint and polymeric sand are what make a patio last 50 years.

We'll bring samples from all three, show you what works with your home, and give you an honest
recommendation for your budget — then install it on a base built to last.
